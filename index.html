Privacy Policy for Tuck

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Overview

Tuck is a pre-departure checklist app that keeps all your data on your device. We don't collect, store, or access any of your information.

Data Storage

All your data stays on your device only:

  • Checklist items — stored locally
  • Lists — any lists you create are stored locally
  • Photos — stored locally, auto-delete after 24 hours
  • Preferences — stored locally (color theme, view mode, "have it" mode, etc.)

We have no servers, no cloud accounts, and no ability to access your data.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't collect your data
  • We don't sync to cloud services
  • We don't share your data with anyone — sharing only happens if you choose /li>
  • We don't track you
  • We don't show you ads

Photos

Photos you capture are stored on your device only. They automatically delete afally delete them anytime.

Sharing

You can choose to share a photo of a tucked item using Apple's native share sheet — for example, to send it via Messages, AirDrop, Mail, or save it to Photos. This only happens when you tap the share button yourself. Once you share a photo, where it goes is controlled by you and vice (including any third-party apps you choose from the share sheet) — not by Tuck. We don't see, log, or retain any record of what you share or with whom.

Third-Party Services
